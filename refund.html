Refund policy

Frequency & Co. LLC · Last updated November 1, 2026

In short. You can try all of CamFX free for 7 days before you pay. Because of that, buying it is a final decision: we don't give refunds after a purchase. If something doesn't work, write to us and we'll help.

Try it first

Every copy of CamFX can be tried free for 7 days, with everything switched on and no payment details. Please use the trial to check that CamFX works on your Mac, with your camera and in your music software, before you buy.

No refunds after you buy

Once you have bought a license, the sale is final. We don't offer refunds for a change of mind, for not using the license, or because you didn't try the trial first.

If something doesn't work

A problem with CamFX is not a reason to lose your money, and not a reason to be left alone with it either. Write to support@frequencycoapp.com, say what Mac, macOS version and music software you use, and what happens. We'll look into it and do our best to fix it.

Mistakes we will put right

  • If you were charged twice for the same order, or by mistake, we will correct it and the extra payment is returned.
  • Our payment provider, Polar, handles payments and has its own rules, under which it can cancel and refund an order, for example if it believes the payment was made in error or fraudulently. Where it does, we accept its decision.
  • If we deactivate a key by mistake, we will restore it.

Your rights where you live

Some places give buyers rights that a policy can't take away, such as in the EU and the UK, where the law may allow a refund if CamFX is faulty or not as described. This policy doesn't limit those rights, and where the law requires a refund, we will give it.

Please talk to us first

If you think something is wrong with an order, write to us before you start a dispute with your bank. We answer, and it is quicker this way.
