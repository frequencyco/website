Reacts to what you choose

Hear the kick.
Ignore the rest.

The plugin listens to the channel you put it on: the master bus for the whole mix, or a single track for just that sound. Pick the part of the spectrum that matters, anywhere from 20 Hz to 20 kHz, and the picture answers it, hit for hit. Try the buttons in the preview above: the same beat, a different reaction. And every filter layer can listen to its own part.

Layered looks

Stack up to five filters, give each its own amount and its own band of the spectrum, and save the combination as a preset.

Made for the mix

The plugin only listens, to whichever channel you put it on. It never changes a sample, so it can sit on your master bus and stay out of the way.

Your iPhone as the camera

CamFX lists Continuity Camera, so your iPhone's camera is one pick away from the camera menu. Use it on macOS Sonoma or later with an iPhone that supports Continuity Camera.

Smooth and fast

A direct camera pipeline that keeps up with the beat instead of lagging behind it.

A band for every layer

Bass on one. Hats on another.

Each filter layer listens to its own range of frequencies, drawn in its own colour on the meter. Hit the kick and one layer snaps; the hi-hat moves another. All at once, all from the same track.

  • Pick a band. Bass, Kick punch, Snare, Vocals, Hats or the whole mix, or drag the edges to make your own.
  • Follow all. One switch makes a layer react to a hit anywhere in the sound, and remembers its band for when you switch it off.
  • In every window. In FL Studio the bands sit under the picture. In Logic's small plugin window, five numbered tabs pick the layer.

Listen to anything

Your DAW, your Mac, your voice.

The plugin follows the channel it sits on. In the CamFX app, one menu switches the picture to everything your Mac is playing, or to a microphone, so it works with software that has no plugin too.

The plugin

Put it on a track or the master bus in your session. It only listens and never changes a sample.

System audio

Everything your Mac plays: Spotify, DJ software, a browser, a DAW with no plugin. Set the five bands right in the app.

A microphone

The built-in mic, an audio interface, or your iPhone over Continuity. Voice-overs land in your recordings, mixed with your screen's sound.

The camera window

Put your face
anywhere.

When you record your screen, the camera sits in its own window on top. Move it wherever it works for the shot and resize it to any size. What you see is what lands in the clip.

  • Movable. Drag it across the screen, to any corner or any spot in between.
  • Resizable. Pull the corner grip to make it small and quiet or big and loud. There's no cap.
  • Remembered. It opens where you left it, next time.

Record anything

Your screen, framed for wherever it's going.

Record the camera, the whole screen, a single window, or an area in the shape you need. One click to start. In the app, screen takes can include your Mac's system sound and your microphone for a voice-over. Recorded from the plugin, a take carries the sound of the channel the plugin is on.

Works with

Any Mac host that loads AU or VST3.

CamFX installs as an Audio Unit and a VST3 plugin, together with the CamFX app, in one installer. How the picture shows up depends on whether your music software lets a plugin use the camera.

FL Studio

The whole picture and its controls live in the plugin window. Resize it and it opens at that size next time.

Logic Pro

Logic doesn't let a plugin open a camera, so the plugin measures the channel (pick each layer's band on its five numbered tabs) and the CamFX app shows the picture next to your session.

Everything else

If your host lets plugins use the camera, the picture appears in the plugin. If not, you use it like Logic, with the app beside it.

Verified in FL Studio and Logic Pro. Other hosts that load these formats, such as Ableton Live, Reaper, Studio One, Cubase and Bitwig Studio, should work the same way, but we haven't tested them ourselves yet. Pro Tools uses AAX, which isn't supported yet.

Privacy

It stays on your Mac.

Your camera picture, your screen, your system audio and your microphone are processed on your computer and aren't sent to us or anyone else. We don't collect usage data. Recordings are saved only where you choose, and a take you cancel is deleted.

Camera, screen recording and microphone use the permissions macOS asks you for, and only when you choose them. CamFX is signed and notarized, so macOS can check it hasn't been altered. You can turn them off any time in System Settings.
