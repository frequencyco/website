Privacy policy

Frequency & Co. LLC · Last updated November 1, 2026

In short. CamFX works on your Mac. Your camera picture, screen, microphone, system sound and recordings are not sent to us or anyone else, and we don't collect usage data or crash reports.

The one exception is your license. To activate and check it, CamFX contacts our licensing provider, Polar, and sends your license key, a scrambled ID for your Mac, and your Mac's name.

Who we are

Frequency & Co. LLC ("we", "us") makes CamFX. You can reach us at support@frequencycoapp.com.

What stays on your Mac

The camera picture, screen, system audio and microphone are processed on your computer. The plugin reads the audio in your session to find the beat, and passes it through unchanged. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Recordings are saved where you choose and are never uploaded. A take you cancel is deleted.

CamFX keeps a few small files on your Mac, which you can delete:

  • your settings and your license record, in ~/Library/Application Support/CamFX;
  • your presets, in ~/Library/CamFX;
  • a small marker that this Mac has used a free trial (a scrambled ID of the Mac and of the trial key), in ~/Library/Preferences.

macOS asks for your permission before CamFX uses the camera, the microphone or screen recording, and you can turn them off in System Settings at any time.

The license check

When you start a trial, activate a key, and about twice a day after that, CamFX contacts Polar's licensing service. It sends:

  • your license key;
  • a scrambled identifier of your Mac, made from its hardware ID with a one-way function, so the real ID is not sent;
  • your Mac's name, as you set it in System Settings, so you can recognize it in your list of activated Macs; and
  • your internet address, which is sent with any request over the internet.

Nothing else is sent. Polar uses it to tell whether the key is valid and how many Macs have it active, and we can see the same list, the key's status and the Mac names in the Polar dashboard. We use it only to run the license: to enforce the two-Mac limit, and to help you if you have a problem.

Buying and the trial

When you buy or start a trial, Polar collects what it needs: your email address, your name, and, for a purchase, your payment details and billing country. Payments are handled by Polar and its payment processors, and we never see your card number. We receive your email, name, country and order details so we can send your key and support you. Polar's privacy policy describes what it does with your information.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your internet address and the pages you ask for, as set out in its own privacy statement. We don't use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking on this website.

When you write to us

If you email us, we keep your messages and our replies to help you and to keep a record of support. Our email is run on Google Workspace.

Who else sees it

We don't sell your information and we don't share it for advertising. The people who handle it for us are Polar and its payment processors (purchases and licenses), Google (our email) and GitHub (this website).

How long we keep it

We keep order, license and support records for as long as we need them to support you, to keep our accounts and to meet legal duties. Ask us if you want something removed sooner.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and ask us to release an activation or delete a license record. Write to support@frequencycoapp.com. Depending on where you live, for example in the EU, the UK or California, you may have further rights over your personal information, and we will respect them. Information that Polar holds for the purchase can also be handled through Polar.

Children

CamFX and this website are not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Where information goes

We are in the United States, and Polar, Google and GitHub also run in the United States and elsewhere. By using CamFX you understand your information may be handled outside the country where you live.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If we ever start collecting something new, we will say so here first.
